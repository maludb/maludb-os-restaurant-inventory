# Estate survey — what Restaurant Inventory must reuse, read, and make new

## 0. The findings, in short

1. **The rule is binding and the catalogue is current.** `shared-schema.md` (plugin 0.7.0) gives every planned table three outcomes — `read` (another application owns the rows: hold its id, reach it through K7), `reuse` (copy the canonical definition verbatim into our own `db/`, extend only by appending), `new` (nothing close; ours becomes canonical) — and requires the survey in Phase 1 before any `CREATE TABLE` (`~/maludb-os-integration/skills/os-integration/references/shared-schema.md:11-19`, `:41-47`). The catalogue (§4, lines 70-125) was last extended 2026-10-09; it does not yet list a units-of-measure table, a recipe, a POS sale, or a waste log.
2. **The retail Inventory application (`/srv/apps/inventory`) is the canonical stock ledger for unit-counted goods, and most of it fits a restaurant's storeroom verbatim**: `locations`, `inventory_balances`, `inventory_transactions`, `goods_receipts(+lines)`, `inventory_transfers(+lines)`, `inventory_adjustments(+lines)`, `inventory_counts(+lines)`, `reason_codes`, `document_sequences`, `tax_rates`, `attachments`, `notes`, `suppliers`, `purchase_orders(+lines)`, `purchase_order_events`, `supplier_links_secure` (`/srv/apps/inventory/db/008_locations_stock.sql`, `011_purchasing.sql`, `005_…`, `006_…`, `009_…`). Its design records them as "the canonical shape for unit-counted goods" and "the canonical purchase order for a retailer" (`/srv/apps/inventory/docs/inventory-design.md:360-364`).
3. **What does not fit is the catalog and the quantity type.** Inventory's `products` → `product_variants` (SKU, GTIN-14, option values, `size_key`, retail/MAP/cost) is a sellable-goods catalog (`db/007_catalog.sql:100-164`); every quantity in its ledger and documents is `integer` (`db/008:41-43`, `:72`, `:245`, `:474`), every cost `numeric(12,2)`, and there is no unit of measure anywhere — a restaurant buys a case of 6 × #10 cans, counts in cans and half-cans, and uses recipes in grams. Reusing the Inventory ledger "verbatim" is therefore impossible without a schema change to the canonical: the quantity columns must become `numeric`, keyed by an **ingredient** (not a variant), with a unit. That is the one deviation the design must record (and propose back to the catalogue as "the canonical shape widened for measured goods").
4. **Only the two process applications carry units of measure, alternate units and catch weight**: the cidery's `app.units` (volume/mass/count with `to_base_factor`), `app.item_units` (a per-item sack/bin/bushel) and `app.supplier_items.purchase_unit_code`/`to_base_factor` (`/srv/apps/cidery/db/004_foundation.sql:40-63`, `:95-103`, `:121-132`); ProcessCore widens the dimensions to mass/length/area/volume/count and adds `items.catch_weight`, `unit_weight_kg` and a `weigh_tickets` table (`/srv/apps/processcore/db/004_foundation.sql:38-66`, `:154-181`; `005_purchasing_receiving.sql:220-234`). Both live in the `app` schema of an adopted `htmx-php-builder` application, not in a kit application's `public` schema — so there is **no canonical `units` table in the kit family yet**; this application makes one (copying the cidery/ProcessCore column shape, which both agree on).
5. **Recipes exist twice, both manufacturing-shaped, and neither is a kitchen recipe.** The cidery's `recipe_versions` → `recipe_stages` + `recipe_lines` (`qty_per_batch_base` XOR `qty_per_l`, immutable once active, one active per product; `/srv/apps/cidery/db/007_products_recipes.sql:83-144`) and ProcessCore's `process_specs` → `process_spec_steps` + `process_spec_inputs` (`qty_per_base_unit` XOR `qty_per_run`, `consumption_mode` explicit/backflush; `/srv/apps/processcore/db/007_products_process_specs.sql:54-121`) give the right *family*: a versioned recipe with lines scaled per unit of output or fixed per batch, an expected loss, a standard cost snapshot at activation. Neither has sub-recipes (a line names an item, never another recipe), yield-after-prep per ingredient, a portion/menu-item link, or plate cost. **A `recipes`/`recipe_versions`/`recipe_lines` family in the restaurant's words is NEW CANONICAL**, with the cidery's column names kept where the concept is the same.
6. **Nothing in the estate holds a POS sale or a menu.** The only sales-line tables are Inventory's `sales_order_lines` (a retailer's order by variant, `/srv/apps/inventory/db/010_customers_orders.sql:106-129`), the GL's and Consultant Tracking's `invoice_lines`, the cidery's `sales_order_lines` by packaging configuration; every POS mention in the designs is an exclusion (Inventory "Later: … a POS" `inventory-design.md:624`; txtSchedules "POS integrations and sales" not in v1, `txtschedules-design.md:31`). Reservations' `products` is a subscription plan (`/srv/apps/reservations/db/001_pg_schema.sql:73-85`). **`menu_items`, `pos_sales`/`pos_sale_lines` (the imported check lines) and the depletion that follows them are NEW CANONICAL.**
7. **For projection, the restaurant family offers exactly one K7 read today: Reservations' `covers_by_service`** — booked covers per date and service at one restaurant, at most 62 days a call, guests unnamed (`/srv/apps/reservations/helpers/os.php:242-274`; `maludb-os.json:104-110`), which txtSchedules already consumes into `forecast_covers` (`/srv/apps/txtschedules/db/011_labor_forecast.sql:22-32`, `maludb-os.json:310-316`). txtSchedules shares nothing a restaurant inventory may read (its one share is `people: true`, HR-only). There is **no sales-history share anywhere**; the restaurant's own imported POS lines will be the sales history, and `covers_by_service` the forward signal.
8. **The GL hand-off is settled by precedent: shares and exports, never a write.** Inventory declares `sales_closed`, `purchases_received`, `stock_valuation` for the ledger (`/srv/apps/inventory/maludb-os.json:159-185`; `mcp/inv_shares.py:56-84`) and Consultant Tracking "bills and never keeps books" through six shares and one read (`/srv/apps/consultant_tracking/maludb-os.json`; design D10 at `consultant-tracking-design.md:566`). The GL declares **no `reads[]` at all** (`/srv/apps/gl/maludb-os.json` has only `directory.reads`; design `gl-design.md:329-331` "K7 reads: none in version 1", `:415` "inventory valuation" Later), so every provider-side share for the ledger is owed back as a GL item (Inventory's G2 at `inventory-design.md:634`). Suppliers are **this application's own rows** (the cidery/Inventory `suppliers` shape), while the GL's `vendors` is the payable party, read by name when the ledger wants to match (`inventory-design.md:356`).
9. **A restaurant is a kernel site, so this application should be `scopes.kind = "location"`** — the first *inventory* application to be scoped: Inventory itself chose `no scopes` ("locations are records, not walls", `inventory-design.md:141`, `:717`), while Reservations, txtSchedules and Pro Appointments are location-scoped (`/srv/apps/txtschedules/maludb-os.json:127-129`; `/srv/apps/pro_appointments/maludb-os.json`). The mirror tables are fixed (`os_scopes`, `member_scope_roles`, `scoped-applications.md:152-175`), txtSchedules' `sites(scope_id PK, location_id, timezone)` is the catalogue's scoped shape (`shared-schema.md:98`), and a scoped K7 share receives `scope_id` set by the kernel, with the consumer naming the kernel `location_id` both serve (`sms-and-reads.md:51-53`, `:70`).
10. **Category and area exist already**: `inventory` is admitted by both checks since db/172 and db/175 (`/var/www/db/175_k30_applications_category.sql:6-9`; `app/features/applications/queries.php:30-32`), so the catalog row (the next K-item) widens nothing; the business area is `Operations` as Inventory's row (`/var/www/db/172_inventory_catalog.sql:15-20`).
11. **Ports**: the next free set by every file and listener on this host is **internal 8200, records MCP 8856, activity MCP 8857, scratch 9301–9307** (8194–8197 txtSchedules' proof servers, 8198 Boards, 8199 MaluMail; 8849–8855 Boards' and MaluMail's; scratch ranges 8501–9207 taken). Two collisions worth a line in the design: ProcessCore's `config/.env` names `ACTIONS_MCP_PORT=8841`/`ASSISTANT_PORT=8842`, which DocCloud's records/activity MCPs now listen on; Consultant Tracking's `config/.env` reads 8107–8109 where its design pins 8185/8831/8832.
12. **`/srv/apps/restaurant_inventory` already exists** — an empty git repository (no commits) with an empty `docs/build-specs/` — so the repository step of "a new application from us gets a `maludb-os-<key>` repository before anything else" is half done; the origin remote and the first commit are still owed.

## 1. The contract — what a new application must declare, and the Phase 0/1 deliverables

- The installation order (`registration.md:168-181`): manifest → database/roles/migrations/`config/.env` → vhost → services → register (catalog `ours`, application, endpoints, roles via `application_roles_refresh`) → application token → scopes and grants → skills and the action registry → agents proposed → health and a real sign-on proof.

**K6/K7** (`sms-and-reads.md`): texts go to members only through `/api/v1/notify/sms.php` (30 a member a day, 2,000 an application; `:12-24`); a provider admits the kernel's token to `app_roles` and its `shares[]` only, a scoped share receives the provider's own `scope_id`, answers are capped at 256 kB (`:50-53`), `timeout_seconds` 1–60 default 8 (`:68`), the consumer treats `no_connection` as "not approved yet" and degrades (`:70-72`).

**Scoped applications** (`scoped-applications.md`): a site is a kernel location of kind `site` with name, address, IANA time zone (`:94`); the application keys everything by `scope_id`, never the location id (`:131`); the mirror tables `os_scopes` and `member_scope_roles` are verbatim (`:152-175`); every scoped row carries the scope and every query filters by the scopes the caller holds, super-admins never special-cased (`:181`); the structure is the kernel's — "a 'new restaurant' button … sends the admin to the kernel's Scopes tab" (`:184`); agents obey scopes through run facts (`:185`); the proof list is at `:188-193`.

**The build order** (`~/htmx-php-builder/skills/new-app/SKILL.md`): Phase 0 delivers the memory model, the question list (record questions from PostgreSQL, activity questions from MaluDB), the ordered feature list, and the OS decision — catalog key, scope kind, roles and rights (`:38-47`). Phase 1 designs the ENTIRE database after the estate survey, each table with its provenance `read`/`reuse`/`new`, plus the activity log from day one, the MCP tool surface mapping every question to a tool, the action manifest in the kernel's eight columns, a drafted `maludb-os.json`, and **one build spec per slice** (`:49-72`, `:120`; template `references/slice-build-spec.md:9-61`). The checkpoint gate: no PHP before the owner approves schema + tool surface + manifest together (`:72`). Phase 2 the shell with the sign-on kit, switcher and record picker (`:74-83`); Phase 3 slices, the exemplar by the planning model, the rest by workers who substitute and never decide (`:85-95`, `:105-123`); Phase 4 the two read MCP servers (`:97-103`).

## 3. The retail Inventory application in detail

**What it is.** Inventory "owns what the business sells and where it can get it" — the catalog, own stock by location with every movement, external sources with every offer snapshotted, and orders filled from stock or drop-shipped (`inventory-design.md:106-145`). It is unscoped by choice: "multi-store scopes (locations are records, not walls — §3)" is an Extended item (`:141`, `:616`); `maludb-os.json` declares no `scopes`, `category: inventory`, `business_area: Operations`, `grant_standing_departments: false` (`/srv/apps/inventory/maludb-os.json:6-7`, `:158`).

**The catalog** (`db/007_catalog.sql`): `brands` (`:70-81`), `product_types` seeded with mattress kinds (`:93-95`), `products` (`kind` single/bundle, `options` jsonb Size-first, `attributes` jsonb keyed by the settings' `attribute_keys`; `:100-123`), `product_variants` (`sku` unique, `option_values`, `size_key` by trigger through the mattress synonyms, `barcode` normalised to GTIN-14 by `inv_gtin14()` (`:18-41`), `mpn`, dimensions in mm/g, `retail_price`/`map_price`/`cost_price` `numeric(12,2)`, `reorder_point`/`reorder_qty` integers; `:134-187`), `variant_identifiers` (kind gtin/upc/ean/supplier_sku/…, `source_id`; `:193-224`), `bundle_components` (`:230-256`), `price_history` (`:261-290`), `product_images` (`:311-336`). **Verdict:** a restaurant ingredient is not a sellable variant — it has no retail/MAP, no size option, no GTIN as its identity (a case barcode is a supplier identifier at best), and its quantity is measured. `brands`, `product_types` (as *categories*: produce, dairy, dry, bar) and `variant_identifiers` (as *ingredient identifiers*: a supplier's code, a case GTIN) can be copied in shape; `products`/`product_variants` cannot — an **`ingredients`** table in the words of the cidery/ProcessCore `items` (`code`, `name`, `item_class`, `base_unit_code`, `costing_method`, `standard_cost_per_base`, `reorder_point_base`, `min/max_qty_base`, `shelf_life_days`, `catch_weight`; `/srv/apps/processcore/db/004_foundation.sql:154-181`) is the closer fit and should be recorded as "reuse of the `items` family under the restaurant's name" with Inventory's `products` name collision recorded, exactly as Inventory recorded Reservations' `products` (`inventory-design.md:376`).

**Settings and vocabulary** (`db/005`): `inv_settings` carries business facts, feed limits, the size synonyms, the attribute dictionary, defaults for reorder, crawl rates and schedules (`:13-76`); `document_sequences` (`:133-137`), `tax_rates` (`:169-175`), `reason_codes` with `applies_to` (`:197-205`). All reusable in skeleton.

**Stock** (`db/008`): described in §2. Two rules worth copying as rules, not just tables: the balance guard — "a balance is maintained only by transactions" (`:49-60`) — and the immutability guard — "a transaction is never changed or deleted — reverse it" (`:97-109`). The count flow opens from live balances and posts `count_correction` rows (`:484-497`). The read functions in `db/014_read_functions.sql` are the pattern for "one SQL function answers the screen, the tool and the feed": `inv_find` (`:180`), `inv_availability` (`:85`), `inv_atp` (`:150`), `inv_stock_value(as_of, by)` (`:397`), `inv_sell_through(days, by)` (`:419`), `inv_reorder_candidates()` (`:260`), `inv_lead_time_actuals(supplier)` (`:447`).

**Suppliers and sources** (`db/009`): `suppliers` (`:21-52`), `supplier_items` (`:54-66`), `sources` with connector, role supplier/reference, `rate_per_second`, `robots_state`, back-off and pause (`:76-128`), `source_credentials` as libsodium ciphertext (`:130-145`), `source_pulls` with counts and policy (`:147-170`), `source_templates` (`:172-212`), `listings`/`listing_variants`/`offer_snapshots` (`:214-322`), the matcher and `match_proposals` (`:361-545`), `watches` (`:700-736`). The listings/offers/matching half is retail-specific (competitor websites); the `sources`/`pulls`/`credentials` half is the canonical connector frame the POS import should sit on.

**Orders** (`db/010`): `customers` (the GL's shape minus the FK, `:21-55`), `sales_orders` (`:57-104`), `sales_order_lines` with a fulfilment choice per line (`:106-141`), `order_payments` (`:223-235`), `shipments` (`:274-305`). ## 9. Gaps — what no sibling has, and so this application would make NEW CANONICAL

Compared against every `CREATE TABLE` under `/srv/apps/*/db/*.sql` (the two process applications in schema `app`, the rest in `public`):

1. **`units`, `item_units`** in a kit application. The cidery's and ProcessCore's `app.units(code, name, dimension, to_base_factor, is_base, display_order)` and `app.item_units(item_id, unit_code, unit_name, to_base_factor, is_purchase_default)` agree column for column (`/srv/apps/cidery/db/004:40-63`, `:95-103`; `/srv/apps/processcore/db/004:38-66`, `:193-201`); no `public`-schema application has either. Copy the shape verbatim (dimensions mass/volume/count; `case`, `each`, `oz`, `floz`, `lb`, `g`, `kg`, `L`, `mL`, `gal`, `qt`, `pt`, `cup`, `tbsp`, `tsp` seeded) and declare it the canonical for the kit family. **Three units per ingredient** — purchase (case of 6 × #10), inventory/count (can), recipe (g or floz) — is the restaurant's need; ProcessCore's `supplier_items.purchase_unit_code` + `to_base_factor` and `item_units` already express the first two; the recipe unit is any unit of the base's dimension, converted by factor; an ingredient whose recipe unit is in another dimension (eggs by count, used by weight) needs `items.unit_weight_kg` (ProcessCore `db/004:162`) or a density — carry both.
2. **`ingredients`** (the catalog of what the kitchen stocks) — the `items` family under the restaurant's word: `code`, `name`, `category` (item class as data, `db/017:10-23`), `base_unit_code`, `catch_weight`, `shelf_life_days`, `costing_method` (last receipt / weighted average — the cidery's `actual_lot` needs lots, which a restaurant does not keep), `standard_cost_per_base`, `reorder_point_base`, `par_level_base` (new: a par per site is the restaurant's reorder rule), `yield_pct` (new: edible portion of as-purchased), `active`. Per-site par and reorder go in a new `ingredient_site_settings(scope_id, ingredient_id, par, reorder_point, storage_location_id)` — nothing like it exists (Inventory's reorder points are on the variant, `db/007:150-151`).
3. **Recipes with sub-recipes and yields**: `recipes` (name, kind `menu_item`/`prep`/`batch`, `yield_qty_base`, `yield_unit_code`, `portions`, `output_ingredient_id` for a batch recipe that makes stock), `recipe_versions` (the cidery's columns: `version_no`, `status`, `expected_loss_pct`, `standard_cost_total`, `activated_at/by`, `change_note`, immutable once active), `recipe_lines` (`seq`, **`ingredient_id` XOR `sub_recipe_id`**, `qty`, `unit_code`, `qty_base`, `waste_pct`, `consumption_mode` backflush/explicit, `notes`) — the cidery's `recipe_lines` has no sub-recipe and no per-line yield (`db/007:128-144`). Recorded as "reuse of the cidery/ProcessCore recipe family by concept; new canonical for a kitchen".
4. **`menu_items`** (what the POS sells: `pos_plu`/external id, name, category, sale price, `recipe_id`, modifiers as lines) and **`menu_item_recipes`** when one item depletes several recipes (a combo). Nothing close; the Sales CRM's `deal_products` names a product by text with an optional provider id (`shared-schema.md:108`) — a different concept.
5. **POS sales**: `pos_sales` (one check: `scope_id`, `source_id`, `external_id`, `business_date`, `day_part_id`/service, `closed_at`, `covers`, `subtotal`, `tax`, `total`, `raw`) and `pos_sale_lines` (`menu_item_id` resolved from the PLU, `qty numeric`, `unit_price`, `line_total`, `voided`, `modifiers jsonb`); the import lands through Inventory's `sources`/`source_pulls` frame; unmatched PLUs are a queue like Inventory's unmatched listings (`db/009:343` `inv_unmatched_listings`). No sibling has a POS line; the datalake's research reports mention no POS vendor either (grep of `/srv/apps/datalake/docs/research/*.md` for Toast/Square/Clover/Lightspeed found nothing).
6. **Depletion**: `inventory_transactions` rows of `txn_type = 'depletion'` with `reference_kind = 'pos_sale'`, one per ingredient per sale (or per day per ingredient per site, the design's choice), produced by backflushing the active recipe version at the sale's business date — the cidery's `consumption_mode = 'backflush'` word (`db/007:139`; ProcessCore `run_consumables.mode`, `db/008:120`). A `depletion_runs(scope_id, business_date, status, lines_processed, unmatched)` document so a day can be re-run after a recipe correction — new.
7. **Waste**: a `waste_logs(+lines)` document (`scope_id`, `logged_at`, `reason_code_id` — spoilage/over-prep/dropped/comp/theft —, `ingredient_id` or `menu_item_id`, `qty`, `unit_code`, `cost`, `photo attachment`) posting `txn_type = 'waste'`. The cidery's `loss_events` (`target_kind`, `qty_base`, `reason_code_id`, `classification expected/exceptional`, `approved_by`; `db/008:229-249`) is the closest family and its column names should be kept; Inventory's `inventory_adjustments` with a reason code is the other (`db/008:388-420`). Record as "two shapes under one family", waste the restaurant's.
8. **Counts in count units with a sheet order**: Inventory's `inventory_count_lines(count_id, variant_id, system_qty, counted_qty)` (`db/008:469-482`) gains `counted_unit_code`, `counted_qty` numeric, `shelf_order`; a per-site `count_sheets` template (which ingredients, in what order, per storage location) is new.
9. **Theoretical vs actual usage and the period close**: `usage_periods(scope_id, from, to, status)` and a function `ri_usage(scope, from, to)` answering per ingredient opening + purchases − closing = actual, Σ depletion = theoretical, variance at cost — the cidery's `v_batch_stage_yields` is a per-batch cousin (`db/012:116-128`); nothing per period per site exists. This is the application's defining question and the GL's `cogs_summary` share.
10. **Catch weight on a receipt line without a weigh ticket**: ProcessCore's `goods_receipt_lines.weight_kg` "the ticket's net, or declared" (`db/005:205`) is enough — declared weight, no `weigh_tickets` table; an ingredient flagged `catch_weight` is priced per weight unit and counted by piece.
